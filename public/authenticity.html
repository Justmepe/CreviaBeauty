Don't take our word for it

Verify it yourself in 30 seconds

The confidence of a real seller is inviting you to check. Here is how to authenticate any premium fragrance or skincare unit you buy from us.

  1. 1

    Find the batch code

    A short alphanumeric string (3 to 11 characters) printed on the base of the bottle and the box. They must match.

  2. 2

    Run it through a free checker

    Paste it into CheckFresh or CheckCosmetic to decode the brand and production date.

  3. 3

    Cross-check the details

    The decoded brand and date should match your product. Sharp printing, quality glass and matching codes together confirm the real thing.

Know the signs

How to spot a fake, anywhere

Useful whether you buy from us or not. A real seller equips you; a fake seller hopes you never look.

Fragrance & skincare

  • ✓Matching batch codes on bottle and box, cleanly printed
  • ✓Heavy, seamless glass with no bubbles and an even spray
  • ✓Crisp packaging, sharp fonts, tight cellophane seal
  • ✗Missing, smudged or mismatched batch codes
  • ✗Scent fades in minutes or smells sharply of alcohol

Human hair

  • ✓Origin documentation naming the source country
  • ✓Cuticles aligned one direction, minimal tangling
  • ✓Burns to ash and smells like hair, not plastic
  • ✗Melts into a bead and smells like plastic (synthetic)
  • ✗Excess shine and heavy shedding after one wash

The honest truth about batch codes

A valid batch code alone is not proof a product is real. Skilled counterfeiters copy real codes onto fake bottles. Anyone who tells you a code is the whole story is selling you something.

Real authenticity is three things together: where it was sourced, the physical product, and the code. We control the first, guarantee the second, and hand you the third. That is why we can offer a full refund if we are ever wrong.

Questions

Before you ask

Why are your prices sometimes lower than the boutique?

We buy through trusted suppliers of authentic stock and sell online with low overhead, no mall rent and no counter staff. Lower price, same genuine product. A price far below everyone else, though, is a warning sign, so we price to the real market, not below it.

What happens if I get something and the code won't verify?

You contact us, we refund you in full and cover return shipping, no restocking fee and no argument. We then investigate and drop the supplier. Our guarantee only works because our sourcing rarely puts us in that position.

Do you sell testers or unboxed units?

Only when clearly labelled as such, at a lower price, and always authentic. We never pass a tester off as a sealed retail unit.

Is synthetic hair ever sold as human hair?

Never. Synthetic is always labelled synthetic. Human-hair units carry origin documentation, and raw units are single-donor certified by the supplier.
